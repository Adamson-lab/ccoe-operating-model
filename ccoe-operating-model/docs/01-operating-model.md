Diagrams: [01 Operating model on a page](../diagrams/svg/01-ccoe-operating-model-on-a-page.svg), [02 Organisation](../diagrams/svg/02-ccoe-organisation-federated-hub-and-spoke.svg).

## Capability pillars
| Pillar | Outcome | Key products |
|---|---|---|
| Govern | Decisions are fast, recorded and enforced | Cloud Council, ARB, ADR registry, Exception Board |
| Enable | Teams have skills and patterns | Academy, champions network, reference architectures |
| Platform | Self-service, consistent environments | Account factory, golden-path modules, catalogue |
| Secure and comply | Controls are automatic and auditable | Guardrails as code, control mapping, continuous compliance |
| Optimise | Spend is understood and efficient | Allocation, commitments, unit economics |

## Service model
The CCoE runs the platform as a product with a backlog, SLOs and a roadmap. Product teams are customers; champions are the voice of each BU. Engagement tiers:
- **Self-service:** catalogue and modules, no CCoE involvement.
- **Assisted:** office hours, design reviews for new patterns.
- **Embedded:** time-boxed CCoE engineer joins a team for complex migrations.

## Team shape (starting point)
Head of CCoE, chief cloud architect, platform lead plus 4-6 engineers, security lead plus 2, FinOps lead plus 1, enablement lead plus 1. One champion per BU at 20-30% capacity.

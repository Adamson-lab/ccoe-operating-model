# ADR-0007: Federated CCoE: central platform core with BU champions
- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-17
- Deciders: Cloud Council
- Domain: governance
- Related: 0001, 0008
- Supersedes: -

## Context
A purely central team becomes a bottleneck; a purely decentralised model fragments standards.

## Decision
Operate a hub-and-spoke CCoE: a small core owns platform, guardrails, FinOps and enablement; BU champions own adoption and feed demand back. Governance is run by Cloud Council, ARB, Exception Board and FinOps Board.

## Consequences
Speed with consistency. Requires clear RACI and champion capacity in each BU.

## Alternatives considered
- Central-only CCoE: rejected, bottleneck.
- Fully federated: rejected, fragmentation.
